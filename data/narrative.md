## Summary
The service processed 2000 tickets with an open backlog of 27 tickets and only 1 ticket older than 14 days. SLA attainment ranges from 73.4% for P4 to 82.1% for P2, all below the 95% goal. Median resolution times are well within target hours, for example 1.8 hours for P1 versus a 4‑hour target. The reopen rate stands at 7.5% and total escalations total 293.

## Key findings
- SLA performance is below the 95% goal for every priority, with the lowest at 73.4% for P4 and the highest at 82.1% for P2.  
- Access‑related tickets dominate escalation drivers: Access request accounts for 20.4% of tickets and 44.4% of escalations; the access theme shows 409 tickets filed as access requests and 64 tickets filed under other request types.  
- The reopen rate of 7.5% suggests a notable portion of tickets are being reopened after closure.  
- Open backlog remains low at 27 tickets with only 1 ticket older than 14 days, indicating good queue hygiene.  
- Median and mean resolution times increase with priority level, e.g., mean of 89.1 hours for P4 versus 2.4 hours for P1.

## Recommendations
- Review and adjust processes for P4 and P2 to close the SLA gap (addresses SLA performance finding).  
- Implement a dedicated access request workflow and clearer guidelines to curb access‑related escalations (addresses access‑related escalation finding).  
- Introduce post‑resolution verification steps to reduce the reopen rate (addresses reopen rate finding).
