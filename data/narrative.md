## Summary  
The service is operating below its SLA target of 95% across all priority levels, with the greatest shortfall in P4. Reopen rates remain elevated, and a substantial portion of escalations stem from access‑related tickets. While the overall backlog is small, a few tickets are aging beyond two weeks, indicating potential bottlenecks. Data quality adjustments have been applied to improve the accuracy of these metrics.  

## Key findings  
- SLA attainment is 81.1% for P1, 82.1% for P2, 81.8% for P3, and 73.4% for P4, all falling short of the 95% goal.  
- The reopen rate stands at 7.5%, suggesting that resolved tickets are frequently being reopened.  
- Access‑related tickets are a major driver of escalations: 409 tickets were filed as access requests, 471 were AI‑themed as access, and 64 were filed under other request types, accounting for 46.8% of all escalations.  
- The open backlog contains 27 tickets, with 1 ticket older than 14 days, indicating a small but lingering aging issue.  

## Recommendations  
- Address the SLA shortfall (especially P4) by reviewing and tightening processes for high‑priority incidents. *(addresses the SLA attainment finding)*  
- Conduct a root‑cause analysis of reopened tickets and implement targeted training to reduce repeat issues. *(addresses the reopen rate finding)*  
- Streamline the handling and classification of access‑related requests to lower escalation rates and improve categorization accuracy. *(addresses the access‑ticket escalation finding)*  

Data quality adjustments: 5 duplicate tickets removed, 5 tickets with impossible dates excluded from resolution-time KPIs, 8 tickets with no priority excluded from SLA by priority, 6 tickets with no description will be labelled Unclassified.
